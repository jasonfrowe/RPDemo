Star Hopper - a vertical shoot-em-up for the Picocomputer 6502.
Fight through 7 levels of enemy waves and a boss at the end of each.

Keyboard
  Move    W A S D, arrow keys, or keypad (7 9 1 3 move diagonally)
  Fire    any other key (hold for auto-fire)
  Pause   P or Pause

Gamepad
  Move    D-Pad or left stick
  Fire    A, B, X or Y (hold for auto-fire)
  Pause   Start or Select

Holding fire fast-forwards the bonus tally.
